Frequently Asked Questions

Can I order Pad Thai online for pickup?

Yes. Order Pad Thai on our online ordering page and pick it up at 805 Village Center Drive in Colorado Springs.

Which proteins can I add to Pad Thai?

Our noodle menu lists chicken, beef, pork, tofu, mixed vegetables, shrimp, salmon, combination seafood and duck. Check the ordering page for the choices available with your dish. The signature Pad Thai comes with grilled chicken.

What is the difference between Pad Thai and Pad Thai Woon Sen?

Pad Thai uses stir-fried rice noodles. Pad Thai Woon Sen uses vermicelli, also called glass noodles, with the same egg, bean sprouts, green onions and crushed peanuts.

Can I choose how spicy my Pad Thai is?

Yes. Every dish on our menu can be made to your desired spice level. Let us know when you order.

Does Pad Thai contain peanuts?

Our Pad Thai is topped with crushed peanuts. Please tell us about any allergy or dietary need before you order.

What are your hours?

We are open every day from 11:00 AM to 3:30 PM and from 4:30 PM to 8:30 PM. Questions? Reach us at (719) 531-5175.
